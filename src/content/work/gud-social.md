### Variations of Designs for Employers

In my design process, I focus on creating multiple versions to ensure the final product meets the highest standards. For the “Sock Tember” drive promotion, I began by identifying key elements such as colors, fonts, and images that could be modified. I then created several duplicates of the original design, experimenting with different color schemes, typefaces, and layouts.

By presenting these variations, I was able to gather valuable input from stakeholders and refine the designs accordingly. This iterative approach not only enhances creativity but also ensures that the final design is both visually appealing and effective in conveying the intended message for the audience.
