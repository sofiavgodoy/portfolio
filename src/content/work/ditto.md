As a student ambassador for Ditto, I produced approximately 20 TikTok videos for the brand, combining short-form storytelling with my perspective as a college student. My goal was to introduce the app through approachable content that felt relevant to a student audience.

This experience gave me the opportunity to bring my creativity into a brand partnership and explore how my own voice could support promotional content. It strengthened my experience in social media content creation, on-camera communication, and translating brand messaging into short-form video.
