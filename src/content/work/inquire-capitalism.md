### Helping Gainesville’s business history get discovered

Client: Inquire Capitalism, University of Florida

Role: Data Analyst, Research Department · The Agency at UF

Focus: Competitive research, search visibility, and content strategy

Website Link: [https://inquire-capitalism.clas.ufl.edu/](https://inquire-capitalism.clas.ufl.edu/)

![](/images/the-agency/01.webp)

Inquire Capitalism’s Gainesville Business History project brings together local business stories and historical resources. As the project prepared its digital presence, our team needed to understand how people might search for this history and how the experience could make its resources easier to find and understand. I researched the surrounding digital landscape, reviewing more than four relevant sites and resources, including local history, tourism, government, and university sources. I also examined eight search terms related to Gainesville’s business and economic history. The results often led to broader local history or visitor information, revealing an opportunity to make business history more visible as a distinct topic.

![](/images/the-agency/02.webp)

I translated the research into recommendations for page titles, headings, metadata, content hierarchy, and navigation. One focus was using clearer, more descriptive language so visitors could recognize what a database or oral-history section offered before clicking through. The deliverable was a research and strategy report for the client. It gave the team a clearer view of the search landscape and practical recommendations for the site’s content and discoverability. This was a research and recommendation project; I am not presenting later website changes or traffic growth as outcomes of my work.
