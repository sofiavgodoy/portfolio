For a Design-A-thon in the University of Florida’s User Design Club, we were tasked to create an online platform that supported a community purpose or united students on campus. So me and my partner created a website prototype that was made to connect people with their community through nearby cleanups, climate events, and political action, so getting involved feels easy and personal

After 24-hour sprint we were able to make various complete pages for the website and create a prototype for presentation. We won Runner Up for Best Design after presenting to various UX professional judges from companies like Google, Adobe, and Figma.

### Mood Board and Planning

For this project me and my teammate planned out our ideas and divided the tabs at hand, we used Figma and FigJam to develop and research our ideas. We also researched Gainesville needs and wants by asking students something they wished was available on campus.
