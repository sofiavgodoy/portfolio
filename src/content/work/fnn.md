I worked with Fire Neural Network and designed their new website following a rebrand. They wanted to capture a more modern look, attracting new customers. I drafted, researched, and learned many rules of design and worked with the development team to publish this site live, connected to back-end data bases and servers.

The problem: Their old website was only targeting wildfire prevention agencies, so they wanted a new website that showcased all their new utility outage prevention features as well to new clients.

### Planning Phase: Mood Board

Following the rebrand principles, I conducted a deep research on the current pages on their website. Conducted a quick competitive analysis on the main competitors websites, and features they had. Then I talked with FNN’s Executive suite and ran through the consumer journey through the website. We narrowed down to two personas. Mid-age wildfire and utility professionals.

After conducting the research and drafting inspiration from various technology forward sources. I created the Mood Board and Planning Sheet for this project

### Development & Testing Phase

Once I finalized the wireframes and consumer journeys for the website. The design process in Figma began. I took inspiration from technological forward websites hero sections like Tesla, and chose to have a high-quality video as the background of the hero section. We also had agreed to have everything in a slightly bigger size than the average as most of the customer base was older, and have a hard time reading small text.

I included a home, about us, wildfire, utilities, news, blog, detector, and login pages. I worked in a team of two designers including myself. I also worked with the on-site developer to publish this website and include interactive effects I wanted to showcase on the website. Such as hover animations, and an interactive header bar. Lastly, we ran the website through user testings for about 25 individuals. We took notes of their feedback, and quickly iterated the design before publishing.

We completed this redesign and implementation project in 2 months, for a live website.
