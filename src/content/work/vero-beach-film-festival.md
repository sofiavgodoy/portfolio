Problem: Vero Beach Film Festival needed to expand their audience and wanted to attract Gen Z

Website: [https://vbfilmfest.org/](https://vbfilmfest.org/)

Through The Agency at the University of Florida, I contributed research and strategic recommendations for the Vero Beach Film Festival and its year-round parent brand, Film Vero. My work explored how the organization could attract younger audiences and emerging filmmakers while maintaining its relationships with existing patrons and the local arts community. I analyzed the festival’s brand positioning, local audience demographics, competing festivals, and Gen Z engagement trends. I translated these findings into recommendations for accessible ticket options, college partnerships, filmmaker programming, and social content that encourages participation.

A central focus was clarifying how the two brands work together: Film Vero as a year-round home for film, arts, and community engagement, and the Vero Beach Film Festival as its annual flagship event. My recommendations emphasized the festival’s personal filmmaker access and coastal setting as strengths that could help distinguish it. The resulting research report connected audience insights with proposed marketing initiatives and measurement approaches, providing a foundation for the team’s ongoing strategy development.
