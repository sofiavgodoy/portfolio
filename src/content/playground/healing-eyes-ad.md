### Social Media Ad - Graphic

For this digital work I partnered with [Graham Johnston](https://ufl.instructure.com/groups/760141/users/1313127) to create a motion graphic social media post for Instagram, using his product "Healing Eyes"

I used Adobe Photoshop and Adobe After Effects to do this.

### Part 5: Post-mortem

This was a very good practice into creating social media content for a brand that is not your own. It also reinforced my skills in Adobe After Effects with animating certain aspects of the piece. If I were to do this piece again, I would add more animations to the call-out statements, more graphics in the background, and music as well.
