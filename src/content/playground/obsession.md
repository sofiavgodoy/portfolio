### Part 2 - Editing Process

I started my editing process using Photoshop. I started on the iPad version and added all the pictured layers as shown above. Then, I clipped the main subject picture of Nicky and had the landscape subject photo of the house porch clipping through.

![](/images/obsession/04.webp)

Afterward, I overlayed another clipped image of Nicky on top of the layers and created a mask to adjust any highlights or features I still wanted to show up in the final render. I repeated this mask process on the subject image as well to hide any distracting features of the car.

I also adjusted the brightness and color balances on all the photos to have a more stylized look overall.

![](/images/obsession/05.webp)

For my final step in Photoshop, I drew on white eyes, and a creepy smile. I did this because I wanted to have those assets in After Effects later to animate.

![](/images/obsession/06.webp)

After this, I moved over the project to After Effects where I started off by animating some keyframes in the Clip of the landscape photo of the house porch. Only moving the picture slightly to have Nicky's silhouette in the center of the frame.

![](/images/obsession/07.webp)

Finally, I added puppet pins to the eyes and smile lines I had drawn on before. Animating them to smile as Nicky centered on the screen. After that, I added some particle line effects and made them red with a high velocity to give them that "horror" feel. Timing them to appear after the full smile.

### Part 4 - Post Mortem

I really enjoyed making this mini project. I have never done a horror themed piece, so making one for the first time was definitely exciting. I also enjoyed learning about Adobe After Effects, and using the positioning tools for animating. If I were to do this project again, I would play around more with the different effects that After Effects offers and test out different timings and grains.
