### Project Timeline and Process

### Month 1: Sketching Ideas and Initial Drafts

### Week 1-2: Concept Development

Brainstormed ideas and themes for the book.

Created rough sketches of the main scenes.

Developed a storyboard to outline the flow of the story.

### Week 3: Initial Draft

Wrote the first draft of the text and story.

Created detailed sketches for each page.

Shared the draft with mentors and peers for initial feedback and opinion.

### Week 4: Feedback and Revisions

Collected feedback on the draft and sketches.

Made revisions to the text and illustrations based on the feedback.

Focused on balancing text and illustrations to ensure clarity and engagement.

### Month 2: Refinement and Finalization

### Week 5-6: Detailed Illustrations

Finalized the illustrations and clay objects with more detail and color.

Ensured consistency in style and theme across all pages.

Incorporated feedback from the initial review to enhance visual appeal and interactive aspects of the book.

### Week 7: Layout and Design Choices

Arranged all illustrations and three-dimensional objects and pasted them on the pages

Chose fonts, colors, and design elements that complemented the story.

### Week 8: Final Review and Submission

Conducted a final review of the book with mentors and peers.

Made any last-minute adjustments and details based on feedback.

Prepared the book for submission to the competition.
