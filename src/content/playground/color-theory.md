For this digital work, I aimed to edit this image into two different times of day. Editing the Color Balances, Lighting, and Brightness. In other words I created 2 value variations in Photoshop using color blend modes, indicating the same location at different times of day. I wanted to try doing a sunset scene to showcase different warm color schemes, and a nighttime scene for a deep blue more monochromatic scene.

Part 1: Here is the original ambient lighting photography. I chose this picture because it had no harsh shadows of values. The trees were a very dark green but did not have direct shadows in the picture. Additionally, I also chose this mountain range scene as I wanted a serene picture.

![](/images/color-theory/01.webp)

Part 4 - Post-mortem: If I were to do this process again I would definitely be more precise with my line work selections. I would also maybe add more detailings to the main focus points. But, I learned how to use clipping masks for editing in Photoshop, and adjust color values in different layers in a non-destructive form. From this process, I really enjoyed feeling like I could change how a scene looks like a time of day just from changing color and lighting values. It gave me more confidence in controlling light in digital media.
